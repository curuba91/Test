In his article the author presents the thesis that our world has become
deeply interconnected through global trade. He gives the example of
outsourcing, where multinational corporations move jobs abroad, and he draws
the conclusion that this development leads to inequality and job loss in
western countries.

The structure of his argument moves from a general thesis to concrete
examples. He uses the metaphor of a "global village" and a rhetorical
question ("Who really profits?") to persuade the reader. The intended effect
is to make the reader question free trade and to appeal to his sense of
fairness.

In my opinion, globalization brings prosperity and cultural exchange, and it
creates opportunities for developing countries. However, it also causes
pollution and the exploitation of workers in poorer regions. In conclusion,
weighing both sides, I would argue that the benefits outweigh the harms, but
only if fair rules are enforced.
